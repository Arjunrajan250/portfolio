Profile

Alappuzha / Kerala / India

I’m Arjun, a frontend engineer working with Angular, React, and TypeScript. At Innspark Solutions, I build user-facing modules for cybersecurity workflows: event investigation, incident response, behavior analytics, and DNS monitoring.

The technical through-line in my work is high-density information. I structure complex views around explicit state, stable alignment, and predictable update paths. Visualization work includes Leaflet maps, force-directed networks, multivariate charts, and Three.js scenes.

Independent projects are smaller systems built to inspect a specific problem: normalizing public government data, keeping finance calculations on the client, vectorizing images without an upload step, or piping media streams without temporary disk writes.
